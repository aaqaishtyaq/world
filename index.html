My name is Aaqa Ishtyaq. I am a polyglot software engineer with an interest in distributed systems, ML infrastructure, and inference engineering.

I have been writing code professionally for 7 years and, as a hobby, since I was 12. I like to read books, take photos, and hike in the mountains near my place.

I am an infrastructure engineer at Deel, working remotely from India on distributed systems at scale. Previously, I was a senior software engineer at HackerRank, building their core remote code execution system.

I am currently deep in ML infrastructure, building for inference engineering, fine-tuning, and training infrastructure. I am building dvito.cloud, an AI lab where I am building products including zei.sh, a distributed microVM sandbox platform for AI agent execution.

The theme of my blog is software engineering, but I tend to derive broader life lessons from retrospectives.
